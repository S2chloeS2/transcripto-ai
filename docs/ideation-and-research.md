#Ideation and Research

PPAM
1. Person ; Who is this for.
2. Problem ; What problem does it solve?
3. Approach ; What solution approach of insight can help solve this problem with gen A
4. Metric ; How will you know if the user achieves their goal?

5. Research ; Google it. Find some other resource(s) to extend your knowledge of the problem - especially how other people are solving it
6. Talk to someone ; talk to someone about the problem (NOT THE SOLUTION)
